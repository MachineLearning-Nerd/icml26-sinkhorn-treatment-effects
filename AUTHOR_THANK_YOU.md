# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Medha Agarwal and Alex Luedtke for developing and sharing
Sinkhorn Treatment Effects: A Causal Optimal Transport Measure. The paper
offers a thoughtful connection between causal inference and entropic optimal
transport.

This repository is an independent, educational evidence audit rather than
the authors' implementation or an endorsement by the authors. The explicit
NOT_REPRODUCED labels for the pathwise claims are intentional: finite
diagnostics should not be mistaken for functional-analytic proofs.
