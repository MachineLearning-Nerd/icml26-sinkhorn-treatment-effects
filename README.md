## Paper

The paper proposes a causal measure based on the entropic optimal-transport
divergence between counterfactual outcome distributions. It studies the
measure as a smooth transformation of counterfactual mean embeddings, derives
first- and second-order pathwise results, constructs debiased estimators, and
uses them for testing. It also introduces aggregation over a regularization
grid and reports Gaussian simulations and PatchCamelyon image experiments.

- Authors: Medha Agarwal and Alex Luedtke
- arXiv: 2605.08485
- Paper page: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08485
- HTML version: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08485

## What this repository contains

The implementation is a small audit of finite point-cloud behavior:

- repro/src/core.py implements direct uniform-marginal entropic OT,
  Sinkhorn divergence, a Gibbs-kernel MMD calculation, and the finite STE
  statistic.
- repro/src/verify.py runs deterministic-seed finite diagnostics C1 and
  C4–C6, and records C2–C3 as not directly reproduced.
- outputs/verdict.json is the claim-level evidence record.
- outputs/gate.json and publication_gate.json are generated by
  repro/src/finalize_gate.py.
- docs/paper.txt and docs/paper.html are the pinned paper snapshot used for
  the audit boundary.

The paper's one-step and cross-fitted nuisance estimators, efficient influence
functions, STEAgg procedure, Gaussian simulation protocol, PatchCamelyon
experiments, and GPU/runtime study are not implemented here.

## How evidence is produced

The evidence path is:

~~~text
repro/src/core.py
        |
        v
repro/src/verify.py  -- deterministic finite diagnostics
        |
        v
outputs/verdict.json -- six-claim canonical verdict
        |
        v
repro/src/finalize_gate.py
        |
        +--> outputs/gate.json
        +--> publication_gate.json
~~~

With NumPy and SciPy available, run:

~~~bash
python3 repro/src/verify.py
python3 repro/src/finalize_gate.py
python3 -m json.tool outputs/verdict.json
python3 -m json.tool outputs/gate.json
~~~

The verifier uses fixed random seeds. Re-running it is a finite diagnostic
check; it does not change the conservative paper-level status.
